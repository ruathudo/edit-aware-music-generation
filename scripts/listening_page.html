Listen, then judge the notes

You will hear short excerpts of monophonic European folk tunes, about ten seconds each at ♩ = 100. Some have been altered and some have not. Please use headphones in a quiet place. The whole test takes about 75 minutes; your answers are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

  1. 1
    Wrong notes, 100 excerpts. Play the excerpt, click every note that sounds wrong to you, or choose "Nothing sounds wrong". Then rate how natural the excerpt sounds from 1 to 5.
  2. 2
    Which version, 40 pairs. Two versions of an excerpt differ at one highlighted note. Choose the one that sounds better, or "No preference".
  3. 3
    Download your answers as a JSON file at the end and send it back.

The blocks show rhythm only: each block is one note, its width is its length, and dashed blocks are rests. Pitch is left out on purpose, so judge by ear. Trust your first impression; replay as often as you like.
