Round 1 · Part 10 / 30

Listen, then judge the notes

You will hear short excerpts of monophonic European folk tunes, about ten seconds each at ♩ = 100. Some have been altered and some have not. Please use headphones in a quiet place.

The test is split into five rounds of about 25 minutes. Do as many as you can; one round is already useful. Each round is a separate download.

  1. 1
    Wrong notes, 20 excerpts. Play the excerpt and click every note that sounds wrong to you, or choose "Nothing sounds wrong". Then rate how natural it sounds from 1 to 5.
  2. 2
    Which version, 10 pairs. Two versions of an excerpt differ at one highlighted note. Choose the one that sounds better, or "No preference".
  3. 3
    Download the round's answers as a JSON file and send it back.

The blocks show rhythm only: each block is one note, its width is its length, and dashed blocks are rests. Pitch is left out on purpose, so judge by ear. Trust your first impression and replay as often as you like.
